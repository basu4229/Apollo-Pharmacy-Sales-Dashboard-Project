# Apollo-Pharmacy-Sales-Dashboard-Project
# 📊 Retail Revenue & Profitability Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

#📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes retail sales, profitability, inventory, operations, and customer performance to identify why revenue is increasing while gross margins are declining.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into actionable business insights through KPI tracking, financial metrics, operational analysis, and interactive visualizations. It is designed from a business analyst's perspective to help management identify revenue leakage, optimize profitability, and improve operational efficiency.

# 🎯 Business Problem
Although revenue has grown by **11.2%**, the business is experiencing a significant decline in *Gross Margin % (32.2% → 27.6%)**.
This indicates that costs are increasing faster than revenue, reducing overall profitability.
# Key Findings

* 📈 Revenue Growth:**+11.2%**
* 📉 Gross Margin %: **32.2% → 27.6%**
* ✅ Return Rate Improved: **3.15% → 1.69%**

Lower product returns improved operational efficiency, but the savings were insufficient to offset the decline in profitability.
# 🔍 Business Analysis
The dashboard highlights several possible reasons behind the margin decline:
* Aggressive discounting and promotional campaigns
* Rising Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Low-margin product mix
* Underperforming stores or sales channels
* Inventory and procurement inefficiencies
These insights help business leaders prioritize actions that improve margins without sacrificing revenue growth.
